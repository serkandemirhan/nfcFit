NFCFit modifications: original PNG files resized and encoded as WebP; separate
192px thumbnails and up-to-640px position images. The modified artwork is also
provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. Original SVGs for selected poses are in source/.
Checksums, source identity and explicit mappings are in the project's
EXERCISE_MEDIA_MANIFEST.json and EXERCISE_MEDIA_MAPPING.json.

The illustrations show positions. They are not continuous movement animations
and have not been certified as professional technique instruction.

UI references: RepDB's MIT example projects informed the thumbnail/list and
position-viewer approach. NFCFit's components are implemented for its existing
React/Vite and Expo screens; no RepDB dataset or premium sample is included.
https://github.com/RepDB/repdb-example-react-native
https://github.com/RepDB/repdb-example-nextjs
